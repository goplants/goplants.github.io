GoPlants Privacy Policy

Effective: on the app's launch date | Last updated: September 26, 2026

“GoPlants” (the "App") handles users' personal information with care and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법) and other applicable laws. This Policy applies equally to the GoPlants app offered on Google Play (Android) and the App Store (iOS).

In short: GoPlants has no sign-up or login. Garden records (crops, care, seeds, stamps, journal entries) are stored only on your device and are not sent to the App operator's servers. Only if you apply for the real fruit delivery event do we receive your name, contact number, and address, through a separate application form.

Article 2 (Purposes of Using Personal Information)

  • Providing App features: Garden records are used solely to run the App on your device.
  • Physical delivery: Event application information is used only for delivering fruit and contacting you about delivery.
  • Responding to inquiries: Inquiry emails are used only for replying and investigating errors.

Article 3 (Retention and Use Period of Personal Information)

  • Garden records: Kept on your device until you delete the App or reset your records in Settings. The operator does not hold these records.
  • Event application information: Kept until 30 days after delivery is completed, and then destroyed (for the purpose of handling delivery disputes). However, if you request deletion before then, it will be deleted without delay, except where it is needed to carry out the delivery.
  • Inquiry emails: Kept until 1 year after the reply is completed, and then destroyed.
  • Where retention is required by applicable laws, information is kept for the period so required (e.g., transaction records for 5 years under the E-Commerce Act (전자상거래법); applicable if paid features are introduced).

Article 4 (Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties)

Since no personal information is collected during basic use of the App, no information is provided to third parties.

For the real fruit delivery event only, information is provided as follows with your consent.

Article 5 (Outsourcing of Personal Information Processing)

We currently do not outsource the processing of personal information. If an online form service is used to collect event applications, the name of that service and the outsourced tasks will be shown at the top of the application form and in this Policy.

Article 6 (Device Backup and Cloud)

  • If Android “Auto Backup” or iOS “iCloud Backup” is turned on, the operating system may back up the App's garden records to your Google or Apple account. This is a feature of the operating system, and its processing is governed by the privacy policies of Google and Apple. The operator cannot access these backups.
  • Files created with “Download save backup” are managed by you. If you share the file with someone else, that person will be able to see your garden records, so please be careful.

Article 7 (Destruction of Personal Information)

  • When the retention period expires or the purpose of processing has been achieved, personal information is destroyed without delay.
  • Electronic files are deleted using methods that make them unrecoverable, and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.

Article 8 (Your Rights and How to Exercise Them)

  • Deleting garden records: You can delete them yourself immediately by resetting your records in the App's Settings or by deleting the App. Because the operator has no copy, deleted records cannot be recovered, even by the operator. Before changing devices, please use “Download save backup.”
  • Access to, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of event application information: If you send a request to the Privacy Officer's email address below, we will process it without delay after verifying your identity.
  • Rights concerning the personal information of children under 14 years of age may be exercised by their legal guardian.

Article 9 (Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information)

  • Minimizing collection of personal information: no personal information is received during basic use of the App.
  • Event application information can be viewed only by the operator with access rights, and is deleted within the set period after delivery is completed.
  • Application forms and inquiries are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Article 10 (Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking)

The app shows rewarded ads only when you tap a “Watch an ad to …” button yourself. There are no ads when the app opens, during key actions such as harvesting or opening boxes, or in the widget. Ads are limited to 3 per day and appear from your 4th day of use.

Article 12 (Children's Personal Information)

The App is a fruit-pot care app that people of all ages can use, and it does not collect personal information during basic use. Only persons 14 years of age or older may apply for the real fruit delivery event; for persons under 14 years of age, the application must be made by their legal guardian, with the legal guardian's consent.
