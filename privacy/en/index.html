GoPlants Privacy Policy

Effective: on the app's launch date | Last updated: October 5, 2026

“GoPlants” (the "App") handles users' personal information with care and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법) and other applicable laws. This Policy applies equally to the GoPlants app offered on Google Play (Android) and the App Store (iOS).

In short: GoPlants has no sign-up or login. Garden records (crops, care, seeds, stamps, journal entries) are stored only on your device and are not sent to the App operator's servers. However, when you watch a rewarded ad, information needed for advertising, such as your advertising identifier, is sent directly from your device to the ad provider (Google) (Article 10), and if you turn on “Local weather,” coarsely rounded coordinates are sent to the weather service (Article 4).

2. Contact us

“Contact us” in Settings opens your email app. The email address and content you send are used only to respond to your inquiry.

Article 2 (Purposes of Use)

  • Providing App features: Garden records are used solely to run the App on your device.
  • Rewarded ads: Ad-related information is used to show the ads you choose and to give you rewards, and is processed by the ad provider (Article 10).
  • Responding to inquiries: Inquiry emails are used only for replying and investigating errors.

Article 3 (Retention and Use Period)

  • Garden records: Kept on your device until you delete the App or reset your records in Settings. The operator does not hold these records.
  • Inquiry emails: Kept until 1 year after the reply is completed, and then destroyed.
  • Where retention is required by applicable laws, information is kept for the period so required.

Article 4 (Provision to Third Parties and Transfer Abroad)

The operator does not receive your personal information directly or provide it to third parties. The two transfers below are sent directly from your device to each provider, without passing through the operator's servers.

Other than the above, information is not provided to third parties except where there is a legal basis for doing so or upon a lawful request from investigative authorities.

Article 5 (Outsourcing of Processing)

The operator does not outsource the processing of personal information. As described in Article 4, the App sends ad data (Google) and weather data (MET Norway) directly from your device to each provider, and each provider processes it in accordance with its own policies.

Article 6 (Device Backup and Cloud)

  • If Android “Auto Backup” or iOS “iCloud Backup” is turned on, the operating system may back up the App's garden records to your Google or Apple account. This is a feature of the operating system, and its processing is governed by the privacy policies of Google and Apple. The operator cannot access these backups.
  • Files created with “Download save backup” are managed by you. If you share the file with someone else, that person will be able to see your garden records, so please be careful.

Article 7 (Destruction)

  • When the retention period expires or the purpose of processing has been achieved, the information is destroyed without delay.
  • Electronic files are deleted using methods that make them unrecoverable.

Article 8 (Your Rights and How to Exercise Them)

  • Deleting garden records: You can delete them yourself immediately by resetting your records in the App's Settings or by deleting the App. Because the operator has no copy, deleted records cannot be recovered, even by the operator. Before changing devices, please use “Download save backup.”
  • Ad-related choices: You can change these at any time in “Ad privacy choices” in the App's Settings (shown only in regions where required) and in your device settings (resetting or deleting your advertising ID, or turning off tracking permission on iOS).
  • Access to, correction, or deletion of inquiry emails: If you send a request to the Privacy Officer's email address below, we will process it without delay after verifying your identity.

Article 9 (Security Measures)

  • Minimizing collection of personal information: the operator does not receive personal information during basic use of the App.
  • Weather lookups and ad communications take place over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Files created with “Download save backup” are locked so that only the App can open them.

Article 10 (Advertising and Tracking)

The App shows rewarded ads only when you tap a “Watch an ad to …” button yourself. There are no ads when the App opens, during key actions such as harvesting or opening boxes, or in the widget. Ads appear from your 3rd day of using the App; seed top-ups are limited to 3 per day, “Invite guests” to 1 per day, and ads that revive a wilted tree to 1 per course.
