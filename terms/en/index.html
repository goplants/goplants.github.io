Effective: on the app's launch date | Last updated: September 25, 2026

Article 1 (Purpose)

These Terms set out the conditions and procedures for using the mobile app “GoPlants” (the "App") provided by the operator of GoPlants (the "Operator"), as well as the rights and obligations of users and the Operator.

Article 2 (Definitions)

  • Garden records: All data that the App stores on the device, including the crops a user grows in the App, care history, seeds, stamps, thank-you gifts, and journal entries.
  • Seeds: In-app points received as a result of caring, harvesting, and delivery in the App. They can be used only to unlock pots, decorations, and crops within the App.
  • Delivery and thank-you gifts: The in-app scene in which harvested crops are sent to the “department store” in the App, and the digital items, such as decorations and signboards, received in the App as a result.
  • Gift card: An image showing a harvested crop and how it was grown. Users send it themselves using the operating system's sharing feature.
  • Real fruit delivery event: An event, announced separately by the Operator, in which real fruit is sent to users who meet the conditions.

  3. Users who do not agree to the amended Terms may stop using the App and delete it.

Article 4 (Description of the Service)

  1. The App is a fruit-pot care app in which users choose a virtual crop, care for it every day, and harvest it after a set period (which varies by crop; 15 days by default).
  2. A day in the App rolls over at midnight based on the device's date. Care missed on past days can be restored only under the rules set by the App (only the missed days can be recovered, using seeds, once per crop).
  3. The App provides a home screen widget and notifications (4 per day by default; the number can be adjusted, or notifications turned off, in Settings).
  4. If you choose “My area” (from a list of cities and provinces, by searching for a city name, or, only when you wish, by your current location), the App fetches the actual weather for that area and reflects it in the garden's weather (rain, snow, wind, heat). Weather information is obtained from an external service (MET Norway, the Norwegian Meteorological Institute, CC BY 4.0), and the Operator does not guarantee its accuracy or connection status. If the connection is unavailable, the App runs with demo weather.
  5. The Operator may change the content of the service (types of crops, care rules, reward values, seasonal effects, etc.) through App updates, and will announce major changes within the App.

Article 5 (Sign-up and Account-Free Service)

The App has no sign-up or login. Garden records are stored only on the user's device, and the Operator does not hold any copy of them.

  1. If you change devices or delete the App, your garden records will be lost. Before changing devices, tap “Download save backup” in Settings on your old device and save the record file (.gpsave, a file locked so that only the App can open it) somewhere you can open it from the new device, such as a cloud drive or a messenger's “send to myself” feature. Then install the App on your new device and select that file with “Import save file” in Settings, and your garden will continue as it was. Importing overwrites the records on the new device with the records in the file (one copy of the records from before the overwrite is kept within the App). If the operating system's device backup (Android Auto Backup, iOS iCloud Backup) is turned on, your records may also be restored with it; however, this is a feature of the operating system and is not guaranteed by the Operator.
  2. The Operator cannot recover garden records that were lost because the user did not back them up.
  3. If an account feature is introduced in the future, the Operator will provide separate notice and obtain consent at the time of its introduction.

  3. The “department store” in the delivery scenes and the thank-you gifts are storytelling devices within the App and have no connection with any actual department store or shop.
  4. The boxes and fruit shown on gift cards are virtual crops grown within the App. Nothing physical is delivered to the person who receives a card.

Once the quantity is filled, reservations cannot be made even if the conditions are met.
  3. Even after a reservation has been confirmed, it will be canceled if the announced completion conditions are not met by the deadline.
